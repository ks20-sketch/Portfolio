Key Projects

Dell–Fraud Prevention Echo System (FPE)

Designed and developed a React-based Fraud Prevention Ecosystem to identify and mitigate fraudulent product replacement requests in Dell's return management process. Built three interactive dashboards covering executive analytics, aging case management, and fraud risk assessment. Implemented duplicate detection logic using customer attributes such as address, phone number, email ID, city, region, and pincode to identify suspicious requests. Enabled agents to prioritize cases based on aging and SLA compliance (72-hour resolution target), resulting in improved operational efficiency, reduced duplicate shipments, enhanced fraud detection accuracy, and stronger decision-making through real-time KPIs and risk scoring analytics.
